Before you call any locksmith

How to spot a trustworthy locksmith

Locksmith scams are common in big cities. Here's what to check, whoever you hire.

They give a full price before starting

Be wary of a low phone quote that grows once the locksmith arrives.

They answer with a real business name

"Locksmith services" with no name often means a call center.

They try to pick before they drill

Most lockouts don't need drilling. Ask before they reach for the drill.

They're local and have real reviews

Look for reviews from real customers in your area.
